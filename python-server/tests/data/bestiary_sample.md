## Introduction

Herein doth this compendium present a most varied assembly of beasts and creatures what may be encountered by those brave souls who doth venture forth into the wild and untamed realms. Each entry containeth complete particulars of the creature's nature, both in matters mechanical and in those of lore and habitat. 'Tis the intent that Masters of the Game might employ these fearsome beings to populate their campaign worlds and thereby test the mettle of adventuring companies most thoroughly.

### Of the Compendium's Use

Each entry of monstrous kind doth follow a most regular pattern:
- **Title Block**: Wherein is displayed the creature's proper name, its Threat Level (being abbreviated TL), and Victory Points (VP) what brave souls might claim upon its defeat
- **Statistics Panel**: Wherein all mechanical particulars art rendered in tables most orderly
- **Narrative Description**: Wherein atmospheric particulars and tactical wisdom art set forth
- **Unique Powers**: Wherein any extraordinary or supernatural capabilities art enumerated in full

The creatures herein recorded span from Threat Level the First unto Threat Level the Seventh, being most suitable for companies of adventurers both novice and of middling experience.

The perpetual clattering of their bones doth oft herald their approach, though 'tis known that necromancers of surpassing skill may silence such rattling through wrappings of cloth or enchantments of quietude.

Most commonly these walking dead art clad in naught but the rotted tatters of such raiment as they wore in life's final hour. With precision most mechanical do they execute the will of their masters, yet possess they no faculty for independent cogitation nor the solving of puzzles. Their movements, though possessed of unnatural swiftness, carry about them such a quality of wrongness as doth disturb the living and make the flesh creep most grievously upon the bone.

### Description

These creatures doth exhibit aggression most constant and possess patience that might be measured in mere moments, preferring violent confrontation to any manner of parley or negotiation. True dragons, those noble wyrms of ancient lineage, regard the venomwing with contempt most profound, accounting them as naught but feral beasts lacking proper draconic heritage. Venomwings will on occasion bend the knee to draconic overlords when threatened with destruction, yet such arrangements prove as fleeting as morning mist.

A venomwing of full growth doth measure eighteen feet from snout unto tail's end, bearing scales that range in hue from muddy crimson unto charcoal gray. Near unto half this considerable length doth consist of the creature's powerful tail, which terminates in a barb most wicked. A specimen of typical proportion weigheth some two thousand and eight hundred pounds, whilst its wingspan doth achieve four-and-twenty feet in breadth. The tail barb extendeth roughly four feet and doth continuously weep venom most potent.

Whilst venomwings possess the capacity to articulate words in the Draconic tongue, they demonstrate intellectual faculties most limited when compared unto true dragons, and show scant interest in discourse or philosophical debate. They establish their lairs within cavern systems throughout craggy highlands, where they make their beds upon mounds composed of bones, plundered treasure, and sundry debris. When hunting in company, venomwings doth employ cooperative tactics most cunning, using their aerial superiority to isolate and overwhelm their unfortunate prey.

### Special Abilities

**Aquatic Breathing (Mundane)**: Tidal pincers extract oxygen from water. They can function on land for 2 hours per point of Stamina. Exceeding this duration causes the creature to suffocate as if submerged in an airless environment.

### Description

These tidal pincers doth exhibit behaviours most similar unto their smaller kindred, consuming with equal relish both the green plants of the sea, carrion most foul, and small fish what venture too near their claws. Most fiercely do these creatures defend whatsoever territories they claim as their own, employing claws of formidable aspect to repel or slay any they perceive as rivals for dominion. The coloration of their shells doth vary most dramatically across the several races and according to their dwelling-places, spanning from drab olive-brown unto crimson most vibrant, or sapphire blue that doth rival the very ocean. Certain varieties bear upon their carapaces intricate patterns what resemble unto geometric designs wrought by cunning artificers.

Whilst the tidal pincer doth require immersion in water to survive for periods extended, they prove themselves most capable upon dry land for brief durations—a quality that doth surprise many who first encounter them. Rocky tidepools and zones of shallow marine character serve as their customary habitats, wherein they scuttle across the seabed in search of sustenance. Upon solid ground these crustaceans art capable of speeds most remarkable, such that they might overtake a running man in sprints of brief duration, much to his dismay.

Communities of fisherfolk do frequently regard tidal pincers as nuisances most vexatious, owing to their propensity for attacking vessels and making raids upon pots set for catching lesser crabs. Notwithstanding this, their flesh doth command prices most premium at market, being accounted a delicacy, and their shells provide material most serviceable for the crafting of protective armour and shields. Hunters of experience doth approach them with caution befitting their danger, for the crushing power contained within their claws is such as can shatter bone as easily as a man might crush an eggshell.

### Special Abilities

**Relentless Determination (Mundane)**: A one-eyed brute remains active and capable of combat even when its vitality drops below 0. The creature continues functioning with limited actions and loses 1 vitality each round, perishing only when its negative vitality equals its Stamina score.

**Moment of Prescience (Mystical)**: Once per day as an instantaneous response, a one-eyed brute can glimpse moments ahead in time. This grants the creature a +10 precognitive bonus on any single d20 roll. This ability activates after rolling, but before outcome revelation.

Alternatively, the brute can expend this ability to gain an additional action during its turn as an instantaneous response (similar to temporal acceleration magic); when used this way, it receives a +3 precognitive bonus to DEF until its next turn begins.

### Description

The one-eyed brutes art descended of a lineage giant-blooded, which hath in ages past deteriorated most grievously from origins legendary unto crude barbarity most lamentable. Those specimens what walk the earth in these latter days concern themselves chiefly with gratification immediate—the filling of belly and the satisfying of base appetites—rather than with the ancestral glories of which their forebears might have boasted. They exhibit hygiene most poor, manners terrible beyond description, and tempers of such explosive character that they will respond to provocations minor with violence most overwhelming and terrible to behold.

Specimens of average proportion doth attain heights betwixt eleven and twelve feet, their bulk weighing some eight hundred pounds. Both male and female art alike devoid of hair upon the scalp, and garb themselves in hides most crudely tanned or fabric of coarse weave that would shame even the meanest peasant. One-eyed brutes demonstrate volatility of temperament most notorious, nursing grudges for periods extended beyond all reason, whilst their interactions with species other than their own doth frequently escalate unto bloodshed and slaughter.

These giants doth organize themselves into clans small and nomadic, making their habitations within networks of caves or structures of stone most primitive in construction. They maintain herds of livestock consisting chiefly of goats and sheep, which they supplement on occasion with travelers captured for service as bondsmen or, when hunger presseth sore, as provisions for the cooking pot. Leadership of the clan doth typically fall unto the individual of greatest physical might, though 'tis known that those who experience dreams prophetic or visions of supernatural character may sometimes claim authority through mandate divine. Their single eye, though it maketh them unmistakable in appearance, doth not diminish their perception of depth, a quality what maketh them yet more formidable in combat.
